Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

our206 is a personal project operated by Ian Foo. This policy covers our206.com and associated Google OAuth integrations used by the operator's Hermes agent and other software under the operator's control. These integrations are intended for personal use and are not offered as a public account service.

Information accessed and its purpose

When the operator authorizes a Google connection, the application accesses Google account data within the permissions granted on Google's consent screen. Depending on the authorized task, this may include account information and content in connected Google services, such as calendar events and spreadsheet data. The application uses this information to perform the operator's requested tasks, including managing and synchronizing event information.

Storage and retention

Authorization tokens, configuration, and task data may be stored in the operator's automation environment. Operational logs may contain task details. Information is retained only as needed to operate the integration, maintain its records, or troubleshoot problems. Disconnecting an account stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete records already written to calendars, spreadsheets, logs, or backups.

Sharing and Google data

Google data is not sold or used for advertising. Data may be processed by hosting, automation, or AI service providers as necessary to carry out an explicitly authorized task, or disclosed when required by law. Google data must not be used to train or improve general-purpose AI or machine learning models.

our206's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy and applicable Google Workspace API requirements, including their Limited Use requirements.

Public calendar and website

Event information intentionally published to the public calendar is visible to website visitors. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and embeds Google Calendar and Google Fonts. These providers receive ordinary connection information, such as IP addresses and browser details, and may process data under their own policies. Authorizing the private integration is not required to view the website.

Control, deletion, and contact

You can revoke Google access at Google Account connections. For questions or requests to delete integration data, contact our206wa@gmail.com. Requests will be handled subject to applicable retention obligations and technical limitations.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the project changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
